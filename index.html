About

Founded in Munich in 2025, H.O.T-Performance is an evolving art collective exploring the role of the sacred in a modern, industrialized world. The group utilizes religious and pagan imagery across various formats, such as audio installations, video art, and public performances. Rather than adhering to a single dogma, the collective functions as a fluid platform where individual motivations vary from purely aesthetic choices and institutional critique to genuine spiritual belief. With no fixed list of members, H.O.T-Performance remains unified by a shared desire to use occult symbolism as a tool for artistic expression and social inquiry.
